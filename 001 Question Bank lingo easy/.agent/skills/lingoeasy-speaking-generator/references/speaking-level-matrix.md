# Speaking: level matrix (config `productive/config/productive_taxonomy.json -> speaking.levels` is authoritative)

| CEFR | band ref | Typical formats | Speaking seconds | Prep seconds | Typed words (min / sug. max) | Demand |
|------|------|------|------|------|------|------|
| A1 | 1.0-2.5 | familiar_topics (3-4 short questions), situational_response (shop, cafe, directions), long_turn (4 simple cue questions) | 30-120 | 0 | 10-20 / 25-90 | present simple, can, question forms; facts about self, family, routine |
| A2 | 3.0-3.5 | familiar_topics, situational_response, long_turn (cue + reason) | 45-150 | 0-30 | 20-40 / 50-130 | past simple, future, comparatives, because/so; short connected answers |
| B1 | 4.0-5.0 | long_turn, long_turn_discussion, discussion, situational_response | 60-210 | 0-60 | 40-80 / 80-200 | present perfect, past continuous, real conditionals; narrate, opine with reasons, give advice |
| B2 | 5.5-6.5 | long_turn_discussion, discussion, situational_response (negotiation, presenting) | 90-300 | 0-60 | 70-120 / 130-280 | unreal conditionals, passive, concession; compare, evaluate, speculate |
| C1 | 7.0-8.0 | discussion, long_turn_discussion, situational_response (persuading, hostile questions) | 120-360 | 0-90 | 100-160 / 170-340 | nominalisation, hedging, mixed conditionals; qualified claims, abstract topics |
| C2 | 8.5-9.0 | discussion, long_turn_discussion, situational_response (mediation, rebuttal) | 120-420 | 0-90 | 110-180 / 190-380 | stylistic variation, concession/understatement, register shifting; precision and nuance |

IELTS orientation (not copied): Part 1 = 4-5 min of short questions on familiar topics; Part 2 = 1 min preparation + 1-2 min long turn; Part 3 = 4-5 min abstract discussion linked to Part 2.
Spoken pace used to sanity-check typed length: A1 70, A2 85, B1 100, B2 120, C1 135, C2 150 words per minute.

Evaluable from a typed transcript: relevance, vocabulary, grammar, development of ideas, organisation/coherence.
Needs audio (not in this MVP): pronunciation, intonation, stress, rhythm, speech rate, pauses, hesitation.
