IELTS anchors (for orientation only, not copied): Task 1 style (letter/report) ~20 min / 150 words; Task 2 essay ~40 min / 250 words. They correspond roughly to B1-B2 demands; below that use shorter, more guided tasks, above it use more abstract content and synthesis.

Placement coverage (per skill, 10 tasks): A1 x1, A2 x2, B1 x2, B2 x2, C1 x2, C2 x1. Assessment: 10 tasks, all at the assessment level, mixing at least 3 task categories and no topic more than twice.

Level demand ladder for the *cognitive* operation (see `cognitive_processes` in the config): A1 describe / recall personal info; A2 narrate, give a reason, request; B1 compare, state an opinion, suggest; B2 evaluate options, analyse cause and effect, persuade, speculate; C1 synthesise viewpoints, adapt register, critically evaluate; C2 hold and defend a qualified stance.
