# Writing and Speaking content audit

Produced by `python -m productive.run_all` (numbers below come from `reports/audit_summary.json`, `generation_summary.json`, `semantic_review.csv`, `human_review_queue.csv`, `test_results.json`).

## What existed at the start
The previous (interrupted) task had already produced **140 parts** (70 Writing, 70 Speaking: placement 10 + A1..C2 10 each, per skill), generated by `productive/` from authored specs, stored in a separate top-level
`productive_bank/` directory with its own layout, with no `SKILL.md` generators and no application support. Snapshot kept in `backups/productive_bank_pre_audit/` (hash list: `backups/hashes_before_restructure.json`).

## Review method (and how far it goes)
Each part was checked three ways: (1) **automated structural validation** (JSON schema, counts, ids, level/profile consistency, ranges for words, time, duration, preparation) - executed on every part;
(2) **automated rule-based semantic checks** (prompt readability caps by level, demand vs. level, criteria relevance, answer-key leakage, duplicate scan, cultural keyword scan, interlocutor lines) - executed on every part;
(3) a **manual read of all 140 prompts, required points and question lists by the authoring model** against the new SKILL.md checklists. (3) is LLM review by the same model that wrote the content, so it is *not independent*; no human has reviewed anything.

## Main issues found (before fixes)
1. Metadata not aligned with Reading/Listening conventions (no `part_type`, `level`, band reference, `skill_tag`) - fixed for all 140 (taxonomy lists extended **additively** in `config/taxonomy.json`).
2. Prompts not self-contained (4 parts) - fixed.
3. Situational speaking tasks showed interlocutor lines as "follow-up questions" with generic instructions - fixed (instructions + UI label "The other person says").
4. Category/format mismatches (2 parts) - fixed.
5. Writing instruction text referred to points "below" while the UI lists them above - fixed.
6. A near-duplicate opener (SP105/SP109) and an identical objective (WR004/WR302) - fixed. Six lower-level near-duplicate *candidates* remain for human review (`duplicate_candidates.csv`, none above the duplicate thresholds).

## Findings that remain (by design left for humans)
* Rule-based semantic review: 73 parts pass cleanly, **67 are flagged** (warnings only, no errors): `cefr_confidence_low` 61, `cefr_estimate_differs_from_target` 26, `grammar_demand_adjacent_above` 5 (intentional hedging structures in B2 negotiation tasks and one A2 quantifier at A1),
  `cognitive_demand_below_target` 4 (narrative or register-rewrite tasks whose cognitive operation is one level below the label).
* CEFR alignment (independent **heuristic**, uncalibrated): exact agreement with the intended level for 57 of 70 Writing and 57 of 70 Speaking tasks, within one level for 70 of 70 each. This is a consistency check by construction (the author chose the demands), not evidence the labels are right.
* Human review queue: 140 parts (P2 = 31 with a one-level gap or warnings, P3 = 109). Nothing is reviewed or approved.
* Metadata completeness: 140/140 parts pass the schema and carry task, expected response, classification, difficulty, evaluation blueprint, diagnostics and provenance.

## Final counts and structure
Writing 70 = placement 10 + A1..C2 10 each; Speaking 70 likewise. Location: `question_bank/placement_test/{writing,speaking}/` and `question_bank/assessment_test/<LEVEL>/{writing,speaking}/`; the old `productive_bank/` was removed after verifying all 140 parts were present in the unified bank.
Reading and Listening files were moved (not edited): all 140 files are byte-identical to the originals (SHA-256 list in `backups/hashes_before_restructure.json`).
